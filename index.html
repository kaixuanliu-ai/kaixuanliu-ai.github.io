Research

My research focuses on learning and decision-making under uncertainty, drawing on large language models, reinforcement learning, and multi-agent systems. I study how intelligent agents learn from experience, reason about their environments, and interact with one another to make effective decisions. I am also interested in healthcare decision-making, including how to support decisions with limited information and long-term consequences. My broader interests include quantum AI.
